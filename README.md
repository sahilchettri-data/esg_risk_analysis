# S&P 500 ESG Risk Analysis

Analysis of ESG risk ratings across S&P 500 companies using Python.

## Tools Used
- Python (Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib)
- Dataset: S&P 500 ESG Risk Ratings (Kaggle)

## Key Findings
- Energy sector carries the highest ESG risk driven by Environmental scores
- Real Estate has the lowest overall ESG risk
- Healthcare leads in Social risk, Financial Services in Governance risk
