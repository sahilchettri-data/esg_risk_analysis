# S&P 500 ESG Risk Analysis

Analysis of ESG risk ratings across 430 S&P 500 companies using Python.

## Tools Used
- Python (Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib)
- Dataset: S&P 500 ESG Risk Ratings (Kaggle)

## Key Findings
- Energy sector carries the highest ESG risk, driven primarily by Environmental scores
- Real Estate has the lowest overall ESG risk
- Controversy level directly correlates with ESG risk — but even companies with no controversy carry baseline risk
- Environment and Social scores are the strongest drivers of Total ESG Risk
- Governance risk is more independent — Financial Services leads despite low Environmental exposure
- Healthcare leads in Social risk, reflecting labor practices and patient rights concerns
